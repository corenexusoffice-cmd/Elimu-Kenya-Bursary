Frequently asked questions

Who can apply?

All Kenyan students currently enrolled in a recognised university or TVET institution who require financial assistance may apply. College and secondary school students are not eligible for this bursary.

Is there a processing fee?

Yes. A small non-refundable processing fee applies per application. The fee depends on your funding band and is shown during application.

How do I pay the fee?

Payment is made by M-Pesa Send Money to the official number shown on the payment page. After paying, submit your M-Pesa confirmation code to complete your application.

How long does review take?

Applications are reviewed after the processing fee is confirmed. You can check your status at any time using your application reference number.

How will I know the outcome?

Once your application is reviewed, your status will be updated on the Check Status page. You will also be notified at the email and phone number you provided.

Do I need to pay again after applying?

No. The processing fee is paid once per application. Approved bursary funds are disbursed separately to your institution or via the official channel.
